# HDVIA Beta 1.25.4.2 — World Data & Media Acquisition

Built on Beta 1.25.4.1 without UI/module changes. Adds seven review-safe capabilities:

1. Global Source Registry & ranking
2. Multilingual Entity Discovery
3. Image/Media Asset Provenance
4. Incremental NEW/UNCHANGED/CHANGED detection
5. Claim Conflict Detection
6. Freshness / staleness tracking
7. World Collector orchestration

## Publication safety
External material remains `CANDIDATE` and is routed to governance. No automatic promotion to `VERIFIED` or live publication.

## Target flow
WORLD → SOURCE DISCOVERY → ACQUISITION → ENTITY RESOLUTION → VISUAL RESOLUTION → KNOWLEDGE FUSION → GOVERNANCE → WAREHOUSE
